# Roadmap

howl is built in **slices**. Each slice is something she can play with; after each one she tests it, we watch, and her feedback shapes the next.

| # | Slice | She can… | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Tune Toy** | Say or type her lines, then tap a tune for each line over a beat, and play it back | **Spec ready** → `specs/slice-1-tune-toy.md` |
| 2 | Words | Say a whole long song, sort lines into verses/choruses, spare lines, rhymes | Wireframed |
| 3 | Beat + Backing | Edit drum grooves, pick chords by feeling, add background sounds | Wireframed |
| 4 | Voice + Mix + MP3 | Record herself singing over her tune, mix, export an MP3 | Wireframed |
| 5 | Cover + Share | Make cover art from her drawing/photo; full Share sheet | Wireframed |
| 6 | Song map + polish | Parts × layers overview, motion polish, first-run, more moods | Wireframed |

Also to design as each slice arrives: motion details, pop-ups and sheets (e.g. New scrap: a bottom sheet with one record button; the app tells hum from words by whether speech recognition finds words, with a chip to correct it), empty and error states, saving/versions, settings, keyboard shortcuts, and sound design (instrument and drum quality).

## First test (slice 1)

A clickable Tune with real sound, before any more polish. The question: **can a non-musician turn one of her own lines into a tune she likes in about five minutes, by tapping pads over a beat?**

## Questions to answer by watching her (not by asking)

- [ ] Does she want to speak a whole song, or write on paper first and type it in?
- [ ] Does "Tap a pad to sing the glowing bit" make sense without help?
- [ ] Tap, Say, Hum or Draw: which does she reach for first? (slice 1 has Tap only — watch whether she tries to sing or hum)
- [ ] Is the guide voice exciting, or does it feel like the app singing her song for her?
- [ ] Does Midnight Fjord feel like hers, or would she rather pick her own accent colour?
- [ ] Is "howl" the right name, or does she want to name it?

## Session notes

Add a dated entry after each session with her: what she did, what confused her, what she asked for.
